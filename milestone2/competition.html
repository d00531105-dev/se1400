Competition

Who is our competition?

Our competition is going to be apps such as All Trails which has a wide database of trails and suggestions for its users. They have GPS abilitys that allow users to track and log their hikes. Obviously, we will not be able to match the level of sophistication as All Trails. However, we don't need to. Our website will focus on giving a more personalized experience to the people living specifically in Southern Utah. This way we can ensure our target audience will get more usage from our website.
