Hiking Webpage

Website Summary:

What content will the site contain?

This site will contain pictures and descriptions of hikes located in the Southern Utah area.

Where will you get all of the content for your site?

I hike a lot and will be taking most if not all pictures myself. I will come up with my own descriptions for the hikes.

Why should users care about the focus of the site?

Hiking is a fun and healthy hobby for people to enjoy. This website will help them find hikes to go on in their area.

Why should a site be built for this?

To help promote outdoor activity in Southern Utah

What would you like users to do at your site?

I would like them to be able to use the site to find a hike that they find intresting and is within their ability to do.

What is the goal of the site?

For me to keep track of my hikes and for others to find hikes for them.
