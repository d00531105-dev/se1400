About

Southern Utah has some of the greatest in America. However with so many awsome trails it's hard to know which ones you should visit, and which would be right for you,

Fortunately we're here to help! This websit contains information from first hand accounts of these trails. That way you can read what people had to say about the trails and pick one that is right for you.

In the navigational menu you can find pages for hikes of varying levels of difficulty. You can also find parks located in Soutern Utah that we reccomend you visit.

Our Glorious Founder,
Evan Matchett

  • For as long as I can remember I have been a fan of hiking. I first started hiking in the forest behind my childhood home and since then I have never stopped. I grew up in Ohio, right at the foothills of the Appalachian Mountians. So, for most of my life I had been solely hiking in Southern Ohio. All of that changed when I went to College.

  • I chose Utah Tech University for a varitey of reasons. One of the major reasons was for the hiking. Hiking in Southern Utah was unlike anything else I had ever done before. The enviroment was so diffrent from Ohio which made each hike feel special. I wanted to go to as many places as I could. After enough hikes I wanted to build this website so I could give suggestions to others living in Soutern Utah, since I feel like their is so many places to go down here it's easy to get choice paralysis.
